## 1. Germany

Germany has some of the richest scholarship ecosystems for international students. Tuition at public German universities is typically **free or very low** (often only a semester fee of €300–€400), making scholarships primarily a living-cost supplement. However, several programs do provide full support.

---

### 1.1 DAAD Study Scholarship (German Academic Exchange Service)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official URL** | https://www.daad.org/en/find-funding/country-specific-information-for-us-applicants/ |
| **Scholarship Database** | https://www.daad.de/en/study-and-research-in-germany/scholarships/ |
| **Value** | ~€934/month stipend + study/research allowance, travel subsidy, health insurance |
| **Duration** | 10–24 months (full Master's degree) |
| **Deadline** | **Late October each year** (typically ~October 15); two deadlines per year (fall and spring). The DAAD portal closes at midnight CET (6 pm EST) |
| **Start Date** | October of the following year |

**Eligibility:**
- U.S. or Canadian citizens or permanent residents; OR foreign nationals whose last degree was earned in the US/Canada
- Currently in the final year of a bachelor's degree at an accredited US/Canadian university, OR a recent BA/BS/MA/MS graduate (degree completed no more than **6 years ago**)
- Must **not** have lived in Germany for more than 15 consecutive months by the application deadline
- A Master's degree program already started in Germany cannot be funded

**Required Documents:**
- Completed DAAD online application form
- Academic transcripts and degree certificates
- CV/resume
- Statement of purpose / study plan
- 2–3 letters of recommendation (academic)
- DAAD language evaluation form (mandatory — must be completed and signed by a German-department faculty member at your institution)
- Language proof: German-taught programs require B2 German (TestDaF or DSH); English-taught programs require IELTS 6.5+ or TOEFL iBT 90+
- Information on your top 3 preferred German master's programs
- Proof of admission (may be submitted after being awarded)

**Essay/Personal Statement:**
Write a detailed study plan (typically 2–3 pages) explaining: why you chose Germany, which specific programs you are applying to, how this master's fits into your career goals, and what you will bring back to your home country or field. The statement of purpose is a critical differentiator.

**Fields:** All academic disciplines

**Notes:** The spring deadline round does not include the "partner university pre-selection" option. Apply at least **two months before the deadline** to gather all materials.

### 1.2 Fulbright U.S. Student Program – Germany Open Study/Research Award

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official URL** | https://us.fulbrightonline.org/countries/europe-and-eurasia/germany/1685 |
| **Apply** | https://apply.iie.org/fulbrightusstudent |
| **Value** | Monthly living stipend (cost-of-living adjusted), travel allowance, health insurance |
| **Awards Available** | ~75 per year |
| **Duration** | 8–10 months (starting mid-September) |
| **Deadline** | **October 6, 2026** (5 pm ET) — 2027–2028 cycle now open |

**Eligibility:**
- U.S. citizen (at time of application and throughout the grant)
- Hold at least a bachelor's degree by the grant start date
- Currently enrolled students apply through their institution's Fulbright Program Adviser
- Not already enrolled in a master's degree program in Germany at time of application
- Open to all academic disciplines

**Required Documents:**
- Statement of Grant Purpose (2 pages) — explaining your project, why Germany, and the significance of your work
- Personal Statement (1 page) — about yourself, your background, and cross-cultural interests
- 3 letters of recommendation
- Transcripts
- **Letter of invitation from a German host institution** (required — must reference your specific project, confirm feasibility, and explain what support they will offer)
- If applying for a master's program: a host letter confirming you meet admission requirements
- Foreign Language Evaluation (FLE) form if German language is required/recommended
- Language self-evaluation

**Essay Details:**
- *Statement of Grant Purpose:* A 2-page, specific, well-researched project proposal. It must name the specific institution(s) you will work with, explain why your project requires being in Germany, describe your methodology, and articulate expected outcomes.
- *Personal Statement:* Demonstrate intellectual curiosity, cross-cultural sensitivity, and your potential as a cultural ambassador.

**Notes:** You may apply to more than one German master's program in case one falls through. Official admission must be confirmed by the start of the grant.

### 1.3 Heinrich Böll Foundation Scholarship

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official URL** | https://www.boell.de/en/applying-scholarship |
| **Application Portal** | https://www.boell.de/en/application |
| **Value** | €934/month (Master's) + allowances for health insurance, family, research travel |
| **Duration** | Up to 2 years (full master's) |
| **Deadlines** | **September 1, 2026** (fall intake) / **March 1, 2027** (spring intake); portal opens ~6 weeks before each deadline |

**Eligibility:**
- International students who have completed their first degree abroad (US degree qualifies)
- Must be admitted to a full-time master's program at a **state-recognized German university** before applying (or be in the application process)
- Priority given to applicants from DAC countries (USA is a DAC country); priority also given to applicants not yet residing in Germany
- Open to all disciplines

**Required Documents:**
- Completed online application form
- CV
- Motivation letter explaining why you are applying to the Böll Foundation specifically
- Proof of German language proficiency: **minimum B2 or DSH 1**
- First-degree certificate (certified copy)
- Social commitment reference (a third party confirming your social/political engagement)
- Expert report / academic reference from a university lecturer

**Essay Details:**
- *Motivation Letter:* Explain your alignment with the foundation's values: ecology and sustainability, democracy and human rights, self-determination, and social justice. Describe your social and political engagement. This is heavily weighted.
- *Social Commitment Reference:* From an organization or individual who can attest to your volunteer, civic, or political work.

**Fields:** All disciplines; foundation values are green politics, ecology, democracy. Candidates who can connect their field of study to these themes have an advantage.

**Notes:** German language proficiency at B2 is required even for English-taught master's programs.

## 2. Switzerland

Swiss tuition fees vary: ETH Zurich and EPFL charge ~CHF 730/semester; University of Zurich charges ~CHF 720/semester; private universities can be significantly more expensive. Switzerland has a high cost of living (expect CHF 2,000–2,500/month for Zurich). Scholarships are competitive and limited.

---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official URL** | https://ethz.ch/students/en/studies/financial/scholarships/excellencescholarship.html |

| **Application Window** | **November 1–30, 2026** (strictly — you cannot apply to ESOP in the second window) |
| **Decisions Announced** | End of March 2027 |
| **Start Date** | Autumn Semester 2027 |

**Eligibility:**
- Rank in the **top 10%** of your bachelor's degree program (self-assessed in application)
- Hold a bachelor's degree or be in the final year of bachelor's studies
- Not currently enrolled in a Master's at ETH Zurich

- All nationalities; all ETH master's programs except MAS

**Required Documents (in addition to the Master's application):**

- CV
- Motivation letter

**Essay Details:**
- *Motivation Letter:* Explain why ETH Zurich, why this specific master's program, and how it connects to your long-term goals. Typically 1–2 pages.
- *Pre-proposal:* A concise outline of the research/project direction you intend to pursue in your master's thesis. The ETH template is provided on the scholarship page.

**Programs relevant to this guide:** ETH offers master's in Management, Technology and Economics (MTE); Computational Science and Engineering; Statistics; Mathematics; Computer Science; and Cyber Security — all of which have economics, data, or IR applications.

## 3. Austria

Austria is a somewhat underserved destination for US students seeking full-degree scholarships. The Austrian government (OeAD) explicitly states it does not offer scholarships covering a complete degree program. The best pathways are through Fulbright Austria or specific university programs. Tuition at Austrian public universities is low (~€363/semester for EU equivalent status; ~€726/semester for others).

---

## 4. Italy

Italy has strong programs in economics, international organizations, business, and arts. Tuition at Italian public universities is relatively low (€1,000–€3,000/year). Private universities like Bocconi and LUISS can range from €10,000–€14,000/year, though both offer substantial merit awards.

---

| | |
|---|---|
| **Official URL** | https://www.esteri.it/en/opportunita/borse-di-studio/per-stranieri/ |
| **Application Portal** | https://studyinitaly.esteri.it |

| **Duration** | 9 months (renewable based on academic progress) |

**Essay Details:**
- *Motivation Letter:* Explain your reasons for choosing Italy, the specific program or research topic, and how it relates to your career goals. Typically 1–2 pages.

**Notes:** Applications are **exclusively online** through studyinitaly.esteri.it. The 28-year age cap for master's programs is a hard requirement.
